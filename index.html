About

Built ISP networks from the core out

I'm a telecommunications professional with over 10 years of continuous experience in Nepal's internet service sector, starting in July 2016. My career has moved steadily from hands-on field networking into advisory and infrastructure leadership roles.

Day to day, that means managing Juniper BRAS/BNG platforms and PPPoE subscriber systems, administering Huawei core switches, and designing Layer 2 networks with VLANs and LACP. I've independently deployed complete ISP infrastructures — core routing, access networks, and subscriber provisioning — end to end.

Beyond operations, I consult on equipment procurement, coordinating with international suppliers of MikroTik, Juniper, Huawei, OLTs and ONTs, and support clients through implementation and troubleshooting.
